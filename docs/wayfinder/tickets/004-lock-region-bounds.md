## Question

What exactly are the Dhaka bus Route (start, end, path, named Stops), the Bandarban hill Route (start, end, any stops), and the two Flight Zone boundaries (centre, extent, spawn points)?

## Resolution

Distances and coordinates are estimates, to be checked against OSM by [Measure OSM coverage for the Bandarban road and Dhaka airport](009-measure-bandarban-and-airport-osm-coverage.md).

- **Dhaka Route:** Farmgate → Airport along Airport Road, under the Elevated Expressway (~12 km). Stops, in order: Farmgate, Bijoy Sarani, Jahangir Gate, Mohakhali, Banani (Chairman Bari), Kakoli, Kuril Bishwa Road, Khilkhet, Airport.
- **Bandarban Route:** Bandarban town → Chimbuk (~26 km), a valley-to-ridge climb. Stops, in order: Bandarban bus terminal, Milonchhari, Shoilo Propat, Chimbuk.
- **Direction:** both Routes run outbound only in v1.
- **Dhaka Flight Zone:** 30 × 30 km square centred near Tejgaon. It covers Uttara and the airport, the Buriganga river and the whole Dhaka Route. Spawn on runway 14 at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport.
- **Bandarban Flight Zone:** 40 × 40 km, covering Bandarban town, Chimbuk, Nilgiri and the Sangu valley. Spawn airborne at ~600 m over the town, heading south.
- **Deferred to Not yet specified:** return-direction runs; extending the Bandarban Flight Zone east to Boga Lake and Keokradong.
