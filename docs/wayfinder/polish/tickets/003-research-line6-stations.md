## Question

Every Line 6 station except Farmgate uses one generic baked platform. What actually differs between the 16 stations: side vs island platforms, concourse levels, roof shape and colour, length, curves, nearby landmarks, signage style? Build on [metro-line-6.md](../../../research/metro-line-6.md) (don't repeat it), using OSM, official DMTCL material and freely viewable photos. The answer groups stations into a few types, so later tickets can choose how many variants to model.

## Research

[line6-stations.md](../../../research/line6-stations.md). The OSM track spacing (4–8 m at every through station), Commons photos and Wikipedia's own Farmgate infobox point to **side platforms at all 15 through stations**. Wikipedia's list of five island stations is unsupported. Uttara North is the exception: it has 4 tracks and the widest building, and its layout still needs a rider's check. Every station is about 180 m long (OSM outlines 178–185 m), has street, concourse and platform levels with the concourse straddling the road, half-height PSDs, and only gentle curves (R ≥ 1.2 km). They share one look: terracotta panel walls, an arched grey steel roof with a green edge, white entrance fascias with the DMTCL logo, and green roundels on the platforms. That suggests four types: A standard (12 stations); B standard with an "iconic" arch at the roof end (Uttara Center, Bijoy Sarani, and possibly others); C the Uttara North terminus; and D the Farmgate hero, whose current white barrel-roof model does not match the photos (teal roof, grid façade, skywalks).

## Resolution

Four station types: A standard (12 stations), B standard with an end arch, C the Uttara North terminus and D the Farmgate hero. All through stations have side platforms and are about 180 m long, with a shared look (terracotta panels, an arched grey roof with a green edge). Both the generic baked roof and the Farmgate hero model differ from the photos. The user still needs to confirm Uttara North's layout as a rider.
