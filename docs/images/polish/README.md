For ticket [Capture in-game vs real side-by-side comparisons](../../wayfinder/polish/tickets/005-capture-side-by-side-comparisons.md). Captured 2026-10-31 at 1280×720 from the dev build (`?time=midday&weather=clear`), from the default cab view, with the bus or train placed at evenly spaced points along each place's baked path. ## Photo checklist for the user

Every row above is marked 📷, because no free reference is current: the Dhaka KartaView frames are from 2019 and predate the elevated expressway, and Line 6's predate its opening. For each spot:

- **Bus Route and Bandarban:** stand on the left footpath or sit at the front of a bus. Look forward along the direction of travel (Dhaka northbound towards the Airport; Bandarban uphill towards Chimbuk). Shoot landscape at eye height, around midday if you can.
- **Line 6:** at stations, stand at the Motijheel end of the Motijheel-bound platform and look along the track, then take one photo of the station from the street below. For viaduct points, take one from the train's front window if you can see out, and one from the street underneath.
- Name each file like the in-game shot (`dhaka-05-real.jpg`, `line6-08-real.jpg`, `line6-08-street.jpg`) and drop it in this folder. Phone photos are fine, and near the point is close enough.
- If you can't reach a spot, a sentence from memory on what's wrong in the in-game shot (too tall, too empty, wrong colour, missing flyover…) also works for ranking.
